Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Passly (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms, do not access or use Passly.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information from Roblox

When you log in with Roblox, we receive the following information from Roblox’s OAuth 2.0 service:

  • Your Roblox user ID (unique identifier)
  • Your Roblox username and display name
  • Your avatar headshot image URL

We do not receive your password or any financial information.

1.2 Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile settings: Custom display name, board visibility, online status preference.
  • Gamepass board: Asset IDs and prices of gamepasses you add to your donation board.
  • Room creations: Room names, descriptions, and type (Public/Private/VIP).
  • Chat messages: Text messages sent in rooms (filtered for bad words).
  • Advertisements: Optional message text when purchasing an ad.
  • Reports: When you report another user, we store your reason and the reported user’s ID.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Donation records: Donor, receiver, gamepass ID, amount, timestamp, room ID (if applicable).
  • Room activity: When you join or leave a room, we update the room’s player list.
  • Usage logs: IP addresses, browser type, access times, and pages visited (via server logs).
  • Last seen timestamp: The last time you made an authenticated request or used a socket connection.

1.4 Guest Users

If you continue as a guest, we generate a random guest ID and store a minimal profile (guest username). Guests can send chat messages with a 25‑second cooldown but cannot donate or create rooms.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, display your profile, and authenticate your requests.
  • To operate room chat, voice chat, member lists, leaderboards, and donation verification.
  • To process and verify donations by checking your Roblox inventory.
  • To display your donation history and totals on your profile and public leaderboards.
  • To moderate content – filter bad words, process user reports, and enforce bans.
  • To communicate important service updates (e.g., changes to terms).
  • To improve our service – analyse usage patterns, fix bugs, and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, the following information is publicly visible to other users:

  • Your Roblox username (or custom display name) and avatar – in room member lists, chat messages, leaderboards, and your profile.
  • Your donation amounts (total received/given) and leaderboard rankings.
  • Your donation history (when you donate to someone, the donation amount may appear in leaderboards).
  • Your board gamepasses (if you choose to show your board).

We may also disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Passly, our users, or the public.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in a MongoDB database hosted on MongoDB Atlas, which is encrypted at rest.
  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS (SSL/TLS).
  • Your Roblox OAuth token is stored only in your browser’s localStorage – not on our server. We only use it to verify your identity on subsequent requests.
  • We use industry‑standard measures to protect against unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction of your data.
  • However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. You use Passly at your own risk.

5. Data Retention

  • Donation records are kept permanently for leaderboard accuracy and to resolve disputes.
  • Chat messages are not stored permanently – they exist only in memory while a room is active. Once a room is deleted (after 12 hours of inactivity or by admin), all chat history is lost.
  • User accounts remain active until you request deletion. To delete your account, email passlyroblox@gmail.com from your registered Roblox account. We will remove your profile, board, and donation records within 30 days.
  • Server logs are retained for 30 days for security and debugging.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & Correction: You can view and edit your profile settings (display name, board visibility, etc.) in your profile page.
  • Delete Account: You may request account deletion by emailing passlyroblox@gmail.com.
  • Opt‑out of leaderboards: Your donation totals are always public; there is no opt‑out at this time.
  • Cookie Consent: Passly does not use third‑party tracking cookies. Essential cookies (for session management) are not used – we rely on JWT tokens stored in localStorage.

7. Children’s Privacy

Passly is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. By using Passly, you confirm that you are at least 13 years old (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the “Last updated” date. Your continued use of Passly after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
