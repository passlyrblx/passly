Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Passly collects, uses, stores, and protects information while operating donation rooms, boards, chat, rewards, leaderboards, and moderation tools.

1. Information We Collect

  • Roblox OAuth/profile data: Roblox user ID, username, display name, avatar URL, and authentication data needed to verify your identity.
  • Discord OAuth/profile data: Discord user ID, username, avatar, and guild list when you choose Discord login or link Discord in your profile.
  • Profile and board data: Custom display name, public tag, preferences, gamepass IDs, gamepass amounts, board visibility, and booth theme selections.
  • Donation and economy data: Donor, receiver, gamepass ID, amount, timestamps, verification status, consumed-purchase records, coin balances, coin rewards, daily reward streaks, and booth purchases.
  • Rooms and communications: Room names, descriptions, membership state, chat messages while rooms are active, private messages, reports, and moderation actions.
  • Technical logs: IP address, browser/device metadata, request path, timestamps, errors, rate-limit events, and security-relevant activity.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users with Roblox or Discord and provide account, room, board, donation, friend, leaderboard, coin, reward, and booth features.
  • Verify Roblox gamepass ownership and prevent duplicate donation credits, duplicate rewards, fraud, spam, and abuse.
  • Moderate rooms, chat, reports, and profiles, including automated filtering and enforcement actions.
  • Maintain service security, debug errors, analyze reliability, and comply with legal or platform safety obligations.

3. Public Information

Your display name, Roblox username, Discord username/avatar when enabled, avatar, public tag, board gamepasses if shown, donation totals, donation events, leaderboard rank, streak leaderboard status, and room participation may be visible to other users.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information when required by law, to protect users or Passly, to investigate fraud or abuse, or with service providers that help operate hosting, databases, logs, and infrastructure.

5. Roblox-Related Data

Roblox transactions, gamepasses, avatars, and Robux are controlled by Roblox. Passly may access public or authorized Roblox gamepass information, including gamepass IDs, names, prices, creator or seller information, ownership status, purchase verification signals, thumbnails, and related metadata needed to operate donation features. Passly may call Roblox APIs to verify inventory ownership and display profile information, but Passly does not receive Roblox passwords or payment details. We use Roblox-related data to connect a board entry to the correct gamepass, confirm whether a donor owns the gamepass, reduce duplicate credits, detect suspicious donation patterns, and keep public totals reasonably accurate. We may store gamepass IDs and verification results even after a board entry is hidden so that fraud prevention, disputes, and leaderboard integrity can be maintained. We may refresh Roblox profile, avatar, inventory, and gamepass data when you log in, edit a board, open a room, donate, receive a donation, or when an automated safety check runs. Roblox API results can change over time, so Passly may update displayed names, prices, thumbnails, ownership states, or verification outcomes when newer information is available. If you remove, rename, reprice, sell, transfer, make private, or delete a gamepass on Roblox, related Passly features may stop working or may show updated information after a delay. We do not use Roblox passwords, Roblox cookies, or private payment details to verify donations. Users must follow Roblox Terms of Use, Roblox Community Standards, and all applicable Roblox rules when using Passly features that involve Roblox data. If Roblox limits access to data, changes its APIs, moderates content, or enforces account restrictions, Passly may be unable to complete certain verification, board, room, donation, or leaderboard features.

5A. Discord Authorization Data

When you authorize Discord login or link Discord, Discord may share your Discord user ID, username/global name, avatar, and guild membership list for the scopes you approve. Passly uses this data to link accounts, show optional Discord identity, refresh Discord server perks such as the Fan public tag and bonus rewards, and prevent impersonation or abuse. Passly does not receive your Discord password, private messages, or server message history.

6. Retention

  • Donation, consumed-purchase, coin, booth, and fraud-prevention records may be retained long term for accuracy and abuse prevention.
  • Room state may expire when rooms close or become inactive.
  • Logs are retained as needed for security, debugging, and legal compliance.
  • Account deletion requests may remove profile and board data, but records needed for fraud prevention, disputes, legal compliance, or leaderboard integrity may be retained in limited form.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including access controls, rate limits, secure authentication practices, and database-backed records. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

8. Your Choices

  • You can edit profile settings and board visibility from your profile page.
  • You can request account review, correction, deletion, or enforcement appeal by emailing passlyroblox@gmail.com.
  • You can revoke Roblox OAuth access through Roblox account settings, but doing so may limit Passly features.
  • You can revoke Discord authorization through Discord Authorized Apps; removing Discord access or leaving the Passly Discord server may remove Discord identity features, the Fan public tag, and Discord server perks.

9. Children and Platform Safety

Passly is intended for users who may lawfully use Roblox and online services in their location. Users under 18 should use Passly with parent or guardian permission. We may remove content or restrict accounts to protect younger users and comply with platform review expectations.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this Policy as features, laws, and safety requirements change. Contact passlyroblox@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
